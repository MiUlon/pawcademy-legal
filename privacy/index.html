Privacy Policy

In short

Pawcademy works without an account. What you enter about your dogs stays on this device. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no ads and no analytics. Purchases, and crash reports if you turn them on, are the only exceptions, described below.

What is stored on this device

Your dogs' names, birth dates, sizes, the problems and goals you chose, reminder times, training sessions, progress, habit ticks, program check-ins and their notes, and the app's settings.

Reminders

Training reminders are local notifications created by the app on this device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Backups

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their terms.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; Pawcademy never sees your payment details. To check what you bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous ID, your purchase records and basic device data such as the app version. Your dogs and training data are never sent.

Crash reports (optional)

Only if you turn on Settings > Send crash reports: when the app crashes or shows an error screen, it sends a report to Sentry, our crash reporting service. A report holds the error, where in the app's code it happened, the app version and basic device data such as the phone model and system version. It holds no name, account or advertising ID, and never your dogs or training data. Turning the switch off, or Delete all data, stops them.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every dog, record and setting from this device at once. Uninstalling the app removes them too.
